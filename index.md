## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/). I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia, where I was advised by Prof. [Zezhou Cheng](https://sites.google.com/site/zezhoucheng/).

I received dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science from the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/) and in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/).

## Research Interests

My research focuses on multimodal learning and efficient 3D computer vision. I am interested in reinforcement learning and self-training for models that jointly understand and generate visual content, as well as scalable 3D perception, view synthesis, and embodied navigation.
